Documentation

AI rotoscoping, made clear.

Better Brush is a local-first AI rotoscoping studio for video editors. Paint a few strokes, let the model propagate a clean matte across your shot, refine the edges, and export production-ready alpha — all on your machine.

🔒 100% local — footage never leaves your machine ⚡ GPU-accelerated propagation 🎬 Editor-friendly exports

Stroke-guided AI

A handful of foreground/background strokes on a keyframe is enough. The model builds a matte and tracks it through motion, occlusion and lighting changes.

Edge-obsessed refinement

Dedicated tools for hair, motion blur and semi-transparent regions, with colour evidence that shows exactly where detail is lost.

Built-in quality control

The Matte QC Inspector and QC Coach analyse every frame and suggest concrete fixes — deterministic, offline, reproducible.

02 — Quick start

Your first matte in five minutes

  1. 1. Import a clip

    Drag a video file or image sequence into the timeline. Better Brush reads common edit-friendly formats directly.

  2. 2. Paint strokes on a keyframe

    Pick a representative frame. Green strokes mark the subject, red strokes mark the background. A few seconds of painting is usually enough.

  3. 3. Propagate

    Press to track the matte forward and backward from the keyframe. Watch the overlay as it solves.

  4. 4. Refine where it matters

    Jump to any drifting frame, add a corrective stroke, and re-propagate just that range. Corrections stay local.

  5. 5. Export

    Send a premultiplied or straight-alpha render to your editor. See Export.

03 — Workflow

How a shot flows

Better Brush treats rotoscoping as a keyframe-and-correct loop rather than frame-by-frame labour:

Import→Stroke keyframe→Propagate→Inspect→Correct→Export
  • Sparse keyframes. Work from a few well-chosen frames instead of every frame.
  • Local corrections. A new stroke only re-solves the surrounding range — the rest of the shot is untouched.
  • Reproducible results. The same strokes and settings always produce the same matte.

04 — Mattes & refinement

Edges that hold up

Hair & fine detail

A dedicated refinement pass recovers strand-level detail the base matte misses, without sharpening the whole edge.

Motion blur & transparency

Soft regions stay soft. Better Brush keeps partial alpha where the image genuinely calls for it.

Spill control

Edge colour decontamination removes background bleed so the matte composites cleanly over any plate.

05 — Matte QC Inspector

See problems before your client does

Open with Inspect → Matte QC Inspector ().

  • Source frame on the left, current matte + QC overlay on the right.
  • Link views (on by default) mirrors zoom and pan across both panes; Reset view returns to 100%.
  • Raw-versus-current scores and changed-pixel counts quantify every fix.
EvidenceWhat it detects
Hair/detail lossFine structures missing from the matte
Background spillBackground colour bleeding into the edge
Enclosed holesGaps inside the subject that should be filled
Edge flickerFrame-to-frame instability at the boundary

Reports are shape-safe, use boolean evidence masks, and never raise on empty or mismatched masks. Comparison reports use after-minus-before deltas — “improved” means lower severity.

06 — QC Coach

A coach that speaks your language

The QC Coach is a local, deterministic, offline assistant inside the inspector. It turns complaints into concrete settings.

  1. 1. Describe the issue

    Pick a common complaint or type your own — e.g. “the hair is too smooth”.

  2. 2. Ask

    Get a direct explanation and a recommended setting change, prioritised from the current QC report.

  3. 3. Save & apply

    Store the answer as a custom tip, or apply the suggested profile to the current shot in one click.

The matcher has explicit polarity: “too smooth” and “too sharp” require opposite settings and are never merged into one keyword bag. Answers and recent questions persist locally between sessions.

07 — Cleanup & repair

Remove what shouldn't be there

Beyond mattes, Better Brush ships a texture-preserving repair tool for removing rigs, wires, dust and unwanted objects.

  • Paint over the offending region; the repair fills it with surrounding texture, not blur.
  • Repairs are reproducible and always available — no cloud round-trip.
  • Combine with a matte to constrain repairs to the subject or the background.

09 — Export

Out the door, into your edit

Video with alpha

ProRes 4444 with straight or premultiplied alpha — drops straight into Premiere, Resolve or After Effects.

Image sequences

PNG or EXR sequences for VFX pipelines, with lossless 16-bit alpha where you need it.

Exports preserve your chosen colour space and frame rate, and filenames follow a consistent, script-friendly pattern.

11 — FAQ

Common questions

Does my footage leave my machine?

No. Better Brush is local-first: inference, QC and repair all run on your hardware. No account, no upload, no cloud dependency.

Do I need a GPU?

A dedicated GPU is strongly recommended for interactive propagation. The Performance Monitor shows utilization and VRAM so you can size shots to your hardware.

Which formats are supported?

Common edit-friendly video formats on import; ProRes 4444, PNG and EXR on export. Matte-only renders are also available.

What if the matte drifts mid-shot?

Add a corrective stroke on the drifting frame and re-propagate. Corrections are local — only the affected range is re-solved.

Are results reproducible?

Yes. The same footage, strokes and settings always produce the same matte — essential for revisions and team handoffs.
